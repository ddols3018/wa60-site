WA60 Uploader

A private publishing tool for the Wellness After 60 YouTube channel.

What it is

WA60 Uploader is a small command-line program used by the owner of the Wellness After 60 channel to upload finished videos, set their titles and descriptions, and publish them on a schedule. It runs on the owner's own computer and connects to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not interact with any viewer's account or data.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool uploads videos to the owner's channel, edits the metadata of those videos, and posts comments from the owner's channel. It does not read or modify anything belonging to other YouTube users.
